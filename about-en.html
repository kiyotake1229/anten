Touch to move. But you can't see.

Guide a glowing dot through the gaps in falling black rows. You can move only while your finger touches the screen. But the moment you touch, your eyelids close and the screen goes dark. Lift your finger and you can see again. But you can't move.

Play in the browser

No install ・ No network ・ 20 seconds to 3 minutes per run ・ iPhone / Android / PC

The first 10 seconds

One line of instructions. The moment you touch, you get the "wait, I can't see" and the rule sinks in.

  1. 0sBlack rows on paper, and a glowing white dot.
  2. 1sTouch. The eyelids close. Only a pale afterimage of the rows remains.
  3. 3sRelease. You can see the rows. But you can't move.
  4. 4sPass rows with your eyes shut: +2, +3, +4…

Look, or move

A single rule becomes a decision every second.

  • Move only while touchingSlide your finger sideways to move the dot. Vertical position doesn't matter. Touch anywhere.
  • Touching closes your eyesThe screen goes dark. The rows you saw linger as an afterimage for one second, and stereo sound tells you where the gap was.
  • Release to see. But you can't moveRead the next gap, then close again. The longer you pass rows blind, the more each row is worth.

Rows that reward reading ahead

Creeper rows move only while your eyes are shut. Mirror rows flip left and right while shut. Choice rows have two gaps, and one of them is a detour.

Six ranks

Your best score sets your rank. Rank and score can be shared as a single image.

  • White — the first ember
  • Blue — 100 rows total
  • Gold — 400
  • Crimson — 1,200
  • Rainbow — 3,000

The glowing dot (the ember) changes color with rows played. A daily layout with three tries, a rematch against your best run's ghost, and weekly badges.

How it is built

One file, no network, everything on the device. Fast, light, and identical everywhere.

The app
A single HTML file (about 90KB). No images, no audio files, no libraries. Every sound is synthesized with WebAudio
Where it runs
Browsers (PWA, add to home screen) and an iOS app (Capacitor: haptics, a morning reminder, share sheet)
Fairness by construction
At most two rows in a row require movement; the third always lets you stay. An autopilot runs thousands of rows to verify the generator (node tools/test.mjs, 40 checks)
Data
Records stay on the device. No accounts, no network, no tracking
Release
Published on GitHub Pages; every push runs an unsigned iOS build on GitHub Actions
Making of
Concept, design, code, verification and assets were built together with AI, from first idea to release in two days
